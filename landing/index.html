All money moves in the same five steps. Each step leaves a stamp you can check later.

  1. AskedA client asks for it in their app, or your team types it in.
  2. CheckedA second person checks the money really arrived, then says OK.
  3. SentThe money is added on MT5. For a moment, MT5 and the books are different.
  4. Written downVaultex writes it in the books on both sides, so nothing can go missing.
  5. MatchedAt the end of the day, MT5 and the books say the same. The account is locked.

      How does it connect to MT5?

      Through the MT5 Manager connection. Vaultex reads closed trades, rates and balances, and sees every deposit and withdrawal. The demo uses sample data instead.

      Can the AI move our money?

      No. Ask Vaultex can read the books and get a request ready. A person has to create it, and a second person has to say OK. Every number it gives comes from your books.

      Can someone change the books later?

      No. Once something is written down, it stays. To fix a mistake you add a new entry that undoes it, so the history is never lost. Closed days cannot be changed.

      Who sees what?

      Your team sees what their job needs. Clients see only their own money. Partners see only their own clients and pay.

      Is it really all in Arabic?

      Yes. Every screen, right to left. The language you pick on this page carries into the demo.

      What do I need to try it?

      Nothing to install. Open the demo and you are in, with a sample company full of data. You cannot break anything.
